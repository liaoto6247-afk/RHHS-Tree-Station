Built for young eucalyptus

Check the soil around the root ball before watering. Young trees need moisture while establishing, with good drainage. A wet reading is a reason to check drainage, not add more water. Care guide ↗

A useful guide, not a lab reading

The 0–100 moisture index shows where a sensor sits between its dry and wet endpoints. It is not water content or a percentage of tree health. The initial bands are provisional; calibrate each sensor in your soil below.

Sensor calibration & technical readings

Starting endpoints are dry 650 and wet 300 on your board’s 0–1023 scale. These are placeholders, not a species-specific calibration. For each sensor, record a stable reading in dry soil, then in thoroughly wetted soil using the same insertion depth. Keep the electronics dry. Enter both readings below. The display bands (0–19 very dry, 20–39 dry, 40–74 moist, 75–100 wet) are adjustable-use guides, not scientifically established eucalyptus thresholds.
